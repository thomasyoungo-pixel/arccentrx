Why programs stall

Why do business transformation programs underperform?

What's broken is rarely what's visible. A program can miss its targets because of ownership, capacity, handoffs, or a plan that no longer fits the business, while the status report points somewhere else. More spend and more activity won't help until that constraint is found.

Signs a program needs a second look
  • Technology spending or pilots aren't showing up in day-to-day workflows.
  • The annual plan no longer matches current business conditions.
  • Processes, ownership, or accountability haven't kept pace with the business.
  • Sales have stalled and the underlying constraint isn't obvious.
How we work

How does ArcCentrx get a stalled program moving?

Every engagement runs in the same order: Assess, Align, Accelerate.

Assess

We frame the problem and the outcome you're after before proposing a path forward. You get a problem everyone agrees on, what success means to each decision maker, the root cause, and a clear scope.

Align

We drive consensus on priorities, constraints, and how success gets measured. You get a plan built for your situation and a workplan with named owners and real measures.

Accelerate

We move from decision to delivery with clear ownership and honest tradeoffs. You get the work delivered, with less time between the decision and the result.
