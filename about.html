Founding Team

ArcCentrx was founded by Michael Panzarella, JoLynn Smith-Joyner, and Thomas Young: three executives who were each already doing this work under their own names and brought their collective expertise together as one firm.

Mike Panzarella
CEO | Founder

Mike Panzarella is an internationally recognized leader in management consulting and financial technology. He has built teams and technology for Apple, Walmart, Uber, Best Buy, Citibank, American Express, and many others across banking, insurance, mortgage, payments, retail, and back-office investing. The platforms and products he's worked on have reached over 40 million users, and he's made the product designs, architecture calls, vendor contracts, and build-versus-buy calls that determine whether a system holds at scale.

At ArcCentrx, Mike leads strategy, business development, sales, and finance. Today, he leads the firm's financial technology advisory practice directly.
